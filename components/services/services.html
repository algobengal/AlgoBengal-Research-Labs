Post-Quantum Migration & Assurance

Independent, research-led security consulting for organisations planning a migration to post-quantum cryptography. We help assess cryptographic exposure, design practical migration paths, and evaluate whether deployments provide the security properties they claim.

01

PQC readiness & cryptographic inventory

Discover cryptographic dependencies, identify TLS termination points and classical-only connections, and prioritize migration risks across internet-facing and internal services.

02

Hybrid post-quantum TLS migration

Design and validate staged deployments of hybrid key exchange, including X25519MLKEM768; assess interoperability, fallback behavior, handshake overhead, and rollout risks.

03

Protocol security analysis & assurance

Review protocol design, trust boundaries, authentication, downgrade risks, and the distinction between browser-to-edge and edge-to-origin protection.

04

Cryptographic agility & migration strategy

Assess dependencies involving digital signatures, PKI, libraries, and key-management systems. Prepare prioritized migration roadmaps, technical reports, and proof-of-concept plans.
