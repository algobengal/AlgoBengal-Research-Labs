Our Research

We study the foundations of cryptography and distributed computation: how to protect privacy, distribute trust, and establish security against classical and quantum adversaries. Our work brings together security definitions, protocol design, and rigorous analysis, with attention to the assumptions and efficiency trade-offs that shape practical systems.

01

Post-Quantum Cryptography

We investigate cryptographic constructions and security models designed to withstand quantum adversaries. We examine the mathematical assumptions, security proofs, and efficiency trade-offs involved in building post-quantum protocols.

02

Private Payments

We study how payment protocols can establish transaction validity while limiting disclosure of participants and financial data. Our research examines the balance between privacy, accountability, and computational efficiency.

03

Blockchain & Distributed Algorithms

We investigate how cryptographic protocols and distributed algorithms can reduce reliance on a single trusted party. Our interests include threshold cryptography, distributed trust, and the security and efficiency of ledger-based systems.
