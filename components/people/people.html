Founder & Director
Pratyay Mukherjee
More Information

A Principal Researcher at Hashgraph, Visiting Scientist and Guest Faculty at the Indian Statistical Institute, Kolkata, and Founder and Director of AlgoBengal Research Labs, his research spans cryptography and security, with a recent focus on applications in payments and blockchain. His previous appointments include senior research roles at Supra Research, Swirlds Labs, and Visa Research, as well as a postdoctoral position at UC Berkeley. He earned his PhD in Computer Science from Aarhus University, an M.Tech. from IIT Kharagpur, and a B.E. from Jadavpur University..
